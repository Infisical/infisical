# Logos

All four are single-ink SVGs; an `<img>` cannot recolor them, so pick the file by ground.

- `infisical-mark.svg`: the infinity mark in ink #0E1014. On `surface`, `paper`, `tint` and the `accent` app tile.
- `infisical-mark-accent.svg`: the mark in the accent #E7F256. On `frame` and other near-black grounds (the dark sidebar).
- `infisical-wordmark.svg`: mark and "Infisical" in ink #0E1014. On light grounds, at 18px tall or more.
- `infisical-wordmark-light.svg`: the wordmark in bone #F1F0EA. On `frame`, `toast-bg` and dark surfaces.

The app tile: `infisical-mark.svg` at 20 by 10 on a 28px `accent` square. Keep clear space of at least half the mark's height on every side.
