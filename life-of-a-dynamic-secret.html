Life of a Dynamic Secret

How Infisical mints a short-lived database credential on demand, hands it out once, and makes sure it dies on time. Step through, click any shape, or press Present to walk through the diagram. Hover anything underlined for a definition.

    Follow one lease

    A static secret is stored and read back. A dynamic secret is never stored at all: Infisical keeps only the root credential and a recipe, creates a brand-new database user every time someone asks, and schedules that user's deletion in the same request. This follows one lease on a Postgres dynamic secret, from request to revocation.
