title: "Deliver secrets to Kubernetes"
sidebarTitle: "Kubernetes"
description: "Choose how to deliver secrets from Infisical to the workloads in your Kubernetes cluster."
---

Infisical can deliver secrets to your Kubernetes workloads in three ways. Most teams use the Kubernetes Operator.

<Card title="Kubernetes Operator" icon="ship-wheel" href="/integrations/platforms/kubernetes/overview">
  <Badge color="green" size="sm">Recommended</Badge>

  Syncs secrets from Infisical into Kubernetes Secrets or ConfigMaps, and can restart your workloads when a secret changes. The operator can also push secrets from your cluster to Infisical and issue dynamic secrets.
</Card>

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Agent Injector" icon="syringe" href="/integrations/platforms/kubernetes-injector">
    Adds an Infisical Agent container to your pods that writes secrets to a shared volume, without creating Kubernetes Secrets.
  </Card>
  <Card title="CSI Provider" icon="hard-drive" href="/integrations/platforms/kubernetes-csi">
    Mounts secrets into your pods as files through the Secrets Store CSI Driver, without creating Kubernetes Secrets.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
