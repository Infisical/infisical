Infisical Certificate Management issues X.509 certificates for TLS, mTLS, and device authentication, from your own private CAs or from external CAs such as DigiCert and Let's Encrypt. It also tracks every certificate in your organization, alerts you before certificates expire, and signs software artifacts.

<Warning>
  If you set up Certificate Management before applications were introduced, see [Migrate to applications](/documentation/platform/pki/migration) to move from the earlier project-based model.
</Warning>
