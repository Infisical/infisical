Infisical Key Management Service (KMS) stores cryptographic keys and runs operations with them, such as encryption and signing, so your applications never have access to the keys themselves.

You can use your keys from the Infisical UI, the [API](/api-reference/endpoints/kms/keys/list), KMIP clients, Kubernetes, and Sigstore Cosign.

<Tip>
To learn how Infisical KMS protects your keys, check out the [Concepts](/documentation/platform/kms/concepts) page.
</Tip>
