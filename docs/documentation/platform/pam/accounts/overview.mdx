---
title: "PAM accounts"
sidebarTitle: "Overview"
description: "Store the credentials for a database or server that people and agents connect to through Infisical PAM."
---

An account is a privileged identity on a database, server, cluster, or cloud provider, such as a database superuser or a domain admin, that people and [AI agents](/documentation/platform/pam/ai-agents/overview) use through Infisical. Infisical stores the account's credential along with the details it needs to reach the target. When someone connects, a [gateway](/documentation/platform/gateways/overview) supplies the credential, so whoever connects never sees it.

Each account belongs to one [folder](/documentation/platform/pam/folders/overview). A role on the folder gives access to every account in it, and you can also give someone a role on a single account. Each account also uses one [account template](/documentation/platform/pam/templates/overview), which sets the account type and the rules for its sessions.
