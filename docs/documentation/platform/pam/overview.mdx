---
title: "Privileged access management"
sidebarTitle: "Overview"
description: "Manage and secure access to critical infrastructure like databases and servers with policy-based controls, session recording, and audit trails."
---

Infisical PAM lets you securely manage access to databases and servers. People connect through a browser or CLI, and so do [AI agents](/documentation/platform/pam/ai-agents/overview), running either as the person who started them or as a [machine identity](/documentation/platform/identities/machine-identities). Nothing ever sees the credentials, and every session is recorded and auditable.
