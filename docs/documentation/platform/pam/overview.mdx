Infisical Privileged Access Management (PAM) lets you securely manage access to your databases, servers, clusters, and cloud accounts. It lets you give people and [AI agents](/documentation/platform/pam/ai-agents/overview) access without ever letting them access the actual credentials. Infisical records every session, and you decide who can connect to each account.
