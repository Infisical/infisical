- **Name**: A descriptive name for the connection
- **Description** (optional): A note for future reference
- **Gateway** (optional): The [Gateway](/documentation/platform/gateways/overview) Infisical connects through, if Infisical can't reach the Redfish service directly
- **Hostname**: The hostname or IP address of the Redfish service, without `https://` or a port (for example, `bmc.example.com`)
- **Port** (optional): The HTTPS port of the Redfish service (defaults to `443`)
- **Username**: The login name of the [local account](#prerequisites) Infisical signs in with
- **Password**: The password of the local account
- **SSL Certificate** (optional): On the **SSL** tab, the CA certificate in PEM format that signed the Redfish service's TLS certificate
- **Reject Unauthorized**: On the **SSL** tab, whether Infisical refuses to connect when it can't verify the Redfish service's TLS certificate (on by default; for a self-signed certificate, enter its CA certificate in **SSL Certificate** instead of turning this off)
