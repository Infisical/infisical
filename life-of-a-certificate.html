Life of a Certificate

How Certificate Manager issues one TLS certificate from an internal CA, delivers it, renews it before it expires, and revokes it. Step through, click any shape, or press Present to walk through the diagram. Hover anything underlined for a definition.

    Follow one certificate

    A certificate is always requested against a profile, which decides how it may be requested, which CA signs it and what policy it must satisfy. Once signed, it is encrypted and stored, pushed to wherever it is used, renewed by a daily job before it expires, and published as revoked if it's ever revoked. This follows one certificate for api.acme.example.
